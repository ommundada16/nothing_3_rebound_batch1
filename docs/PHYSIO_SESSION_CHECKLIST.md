# Checklist for the session with the physio

**Ask permission in writing** (a WhatsApp message is enough) to use his voice and image in the
app, the demo and the slides.

## Record in the app (Physio tab)
- [ ] *My voice*: one clip for each of start, push (2 or 3 variants), hold, relax / chhoro, good, keep going, slow down, stop if sharp pain, done. Say them the way he does in the room (Marathi, Hindi or English)
- [ ] *Videos*: heel slide and straight leg raise, once with a healthy person (reference) and once with the patient in their own room with their own pillow
- [ ] *Plan*: reps, hold, pillow stage, precautions
- [ ] *My rules*: read every line; change anything he disagrees with

## Ask him (10 to 15 minutes)
1. Typical journey from discharge to full recovery: where does it go wrong?
2. Who drops out of exercises, and when?
3. How do you tell fear, pain, weakness and scar stiffness apart at a plateau?
4. How do you know a patient skipped sessions?
5. Which warning signs make you refer to the surgeon, and how late is it usually caught?
6. What do you say to a frightened patient?
7. What do families call about, and how many are false alarms?
8. How do you record progress now?
9. Which movements matter most for Indian patients?
10. How do you measure the bend, and how accurate must it be?
11. Can you introduce one or two knee-replacement patients?

## Accuracy check
- [ ] Hold the leg at 6 to 10 different knee angles; read each with a goniometer or protractor; capture each in *Accuracy check*; export the CSV
